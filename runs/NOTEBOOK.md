`delta_mu=4`, `chi=0.7`, `eps=1e-3`, CSTR `D=0.05`, 2 network reps.

0 to ~0.95 somewhere in `p_cat` in [0.4, 0.6] (which combinations of
`p_selfrep` host RAFs is in `summary.json`). The marginal-curve model
selection returns "null" — expected, since the mean over mixed
`p_selfrep` strata is diffuse. This is a pipeline-shakedown pilot, not
a transition claim.
